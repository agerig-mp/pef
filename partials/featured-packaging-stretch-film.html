Packaging Material

Why stretch film matters.

The material is part of the protection strategy, not an afterthought after the load is built.

Stretch film helps unitize pallet loads for delivery. As it is applied, the film stretches around the load to hold product in place, making it a practical choice for operations that need dependable containment without adding unnecessary complexity.

Packaging advantages to consider

Cast Film

  • Multi-layer options for a range of load needs
  • Excellent load-retaining capability
  • Quiet operation and superior film clarity
  • Single-sided or two-sided cling varieties
  • Thinner gauges with consistent performance

Blown Film

  • Available in mono-layer and multi-layer options
  • Strong load-holding capability
  • High puncture and tear resistance
  • High cling levels for secure containment
