The Challenge

Protecting delicate product without slowing the operation.

Eggshell packaging puts pressure on every part of the process. Loads need stable containment, consistent tension, and a process the team can run with confidence as production needs change.

The Solution

A practical stretch packaging setup built around the line.

PEF aligned the conversation around material performance, wrap consistency, and equipment fit, helping the operation create a more reliable packaging routine without overcomplicating the work on the floor.
